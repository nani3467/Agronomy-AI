⚠️ Section 1: The Problem
  • Early Disease Detection: Farmers struggle to identify crop infections early, risking severe yield loss and costly late interventions.
  • Complex Crop Selection: Selecting optimal crops requires intricate soil NPK balancing, pH understanding, and climate matching.
  • Information Barriers: Fragmented access to scientific ICAR agronomy guidelines leaves farmers dependent on hearsay.
  • Digital Literacy & Language Gap: Traditional typing-heavy apps fail rural farmers due to script and literacy barriers.
🌱 Section 2: Our Solution
  • Voice-First Accessibility: Hands-free voice interface in native Indian languages powered by Gnani AI speech engines.
  • AI Vision Diagnostics: Instant leaf disease detection across 38 classes via deep learning MobileNetV2.
  • Intelligent Crop Recommendation: Data-driven soil and climate matching for 22 crops using trained Random Forest ML.
  • ICAR-Grounded Guidance: 5-part actionable reports covering organic remedies, approved chemicals, dosages, and safety PPE.
🎙️ SECTION 3: GNANI AI INTEGRATION — CORE SPEECH ENGINE & ACCESSIBILITY HERO
Verified Backend Vachana REST API Integration
GNANI PRISMA | SPEECH-TO-TEXT
Model: prisma-v3.0
  • Spoken Query Conversion: Converts farmer's raw microphone audio (WAV/WebM) into high-accuracy text at api.vachana.ai/stt/v3.
  • Multilingual Speech Recognition: Built-in Indian vernacular support for Telugu (te-IN), Hindi (hi-IN), and English (en-IN) with auto-detection.
  • Typing-Free Navigation: Empowers farmers to issue hands-free voice commands, navigate pages, and trigger ML workflows without typing.
GNANI TIMBRE | TEXT-TO-SPEECH
Model: timbre-v3.0
  • Spoken Agricultural Guidance: Synthesizes AI diagnosis & advice into natural, human-sounding speech via api.vachana.ai/api/v1/tts/inference.
  • Native Vernacular Voices: Dynamic voice allocation—Lakshmi (Telugu), Shivani (Hindi), and Avinash (Indian English).
  • Acoustic Optimization: Converts 24kHz linear PCM to valid RIFF WAV with broadcast loudness normalization (-14 dBFS) & HTML5 repair.

⚡ Section 4: Key Platform Features
🦠 Plant Disease Detection Verified ML
Upload leaf photos to detect 38 plant disease classes across 14+ crops via PyTorch MobileNetV2 with non-plant heuristic filtering.
🌾 Crop Recommendation Verified ML
Recommends optimal crop out of 22 varieties based on soil N-P-K, pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall using Random Forest (99.5% accuracy).

Comprehensive database of 22 crop manuals and 38 disease dossiers containing symptoms, pathogen info, and seasonal cultivation requirements.
🎙️ Voice-First Assistant Verified
